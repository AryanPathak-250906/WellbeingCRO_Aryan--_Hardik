# Wellbeing Nutrition: CRO Prototype

A concept prototype of a redesigned product page for Wellbeing Nutrition's Korean Marine Collagen Peptides, built for a product management case study on conversion rate optimisation.

**Live demo:** https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/

## What it shows
- **Plan selector:** every pack shows its days of supply, cost per serving and the label's 3–4 month guidance
- **Assurance panel:** delivery check by pincode, COD terms, returns and who should not take the product, shown next to the button
- **Real stock status** in place of a random "Only N left" counter
- A focused mobile buy box with a sticky purchase bar, reviews with text shown first, and one consistent evidence section

Click **Show CRO rationale** in the top bar to see the reasoning behind each change.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step or server is needed.

## Notes
- Cart, checkout, stock status and delivery dates are simulated; no order is placed.
- Product facts and prices were observed on the live site on 21 Oct 2026.
