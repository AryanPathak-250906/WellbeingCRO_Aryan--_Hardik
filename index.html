Studied as an ingredient, not this exact product

Hydrolysed collagen peptides

In randomised, placebo-controlled trials, hydrolysed collagen peptides were taken daily for 8–12 weeks. Pooled analyses report better skin hydration and elasticity than placebo. Higher-quality and independently funded trials show smaller or no effects, so results vary from person to person.

Studies referenced on this page (4)
  • Kim et al., J Med Food, 2022. Low-molecular-weight collagen peptides; 100 adults randomised, 12 weeks; wrinkles, elasticity and hydration improved versus placebo.
  • Evans et al., J Cosmet Dermatol, 2021. Fish collagen from another supplier, 10 g/day, women 45–60, 12 weeks; wrinkle score 35% lower than baseline.
  • Demir-Dora et al., J Clin Med, 2024. A branded collagen peptide, 10 g/day, 112 women, 8 weeks; elasticity, hydration and roughness improved versus placebo.
  • Bianchi et al., J Clin Aesthet Dermatol, 2022. Hydrolysed collagen, 52 adults, 56 days; moisturisation, elasticity and wrinkle depth improved versus control.

When to expect results: the label recommends 1 scoop daily for at least 3–4 months.
